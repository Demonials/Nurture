# Nurture Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Nurture's base IDE and interpreter run locally. They do not require an account and do not intentionally upload source code, editor contents, passwords, or personal information.

Programs can explicitly make network requests through Nurture networking APIs. Those requests are controlled by the program and are subject to the destination service's policies.

The base release has no automatic AI upload. A future optional AI provider may transmit data according to the provider and the user's configuration. Such a feature should clearly disclose what is transmitted before sending it.

Operating systems, websites, network providers, and external services may keep their own logs.

This policy may change as Nurture develops.
